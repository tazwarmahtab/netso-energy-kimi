# Sky hero quality bar

Reference plate: `app/public/assets/v2/frames/00-roof-transform/f001.jpg`.
Reference continuation: `app/public/assets/v2/frames/00-roof-transform/f120.jpg`.
Surface: `/v2`, desktop and mobile.

These are binary, rendered-output checks for the iterative review loop:

1. **Sky-first first viewport:** the opening viewport is dominated by the slate-blue / peach / amber sky plate; the headline is legible without obscuring the cloud structure.
2. **Confident type lockup:** desktop headline resolves to two intentional lines, mobile to a readable three-line stack; no awkward orphan, collision, or weak contrast.
3. **Single cinematic playhead:** the sky holds before the roof reveal, then the photographic rooftop enters continuously; no hard cut, blank frame, competing pin, or seam.
4. **Clear narrative hierarchy:** one hero message, one roof message, one primary assessment action; nav, proof marker, scroll cue, and WhatsApp affordance stay subordinate.
5. **Palette continuity:** shader, poster, veil, type, and gold accent share the existing Netso visual language; no generic gradient or unrelated color temperature appears.
6. **Responsive integrity:** at mobile widths the sky and rooftop remain intentional static compositions, controls remain reachable, type stays inside the viewport, and no horizontal overflow occurs.
7. **Motion and accessibility discipline:** shader and scroll motion pause or disappear under reduced motion / offscreen conditions; interactive copy is not focusable while visually inactive; no console or media errors.
