# Netso Energy / v2 — Directed shot list

This is the first-cut visual direction for the cinematic `/v2` page. Source media is preserved in the chat attachment workspace; web derivatives live in `app/public/assets/v2/`. The page runtime is the sky-first master described in `v2-storyboard.md`; the interior and unused-roof beats remain optional alternate preludes, not page-master scenes.

## Visual thesis

Energy is introduced as a lived condition before it becomes an engineering system. The cut moves from an interior ceiling fan, to an ordinary Dhaka roof, to a designed solar structure, to the people and maintenance discipline that make the asset credible.

## Authenticity rules

- Real field footage remains evidence; concept/generated clips are labeled as concept visualization.
- The site never presents generated technicians or meetings as installed proof.
- Finance numbers remain illustrative until verified by a real feasibility assessment.
- The opening does not fake ceiling-fan rotation. The paired stills create time through light and dissolve instead.

## Motion rules

- Scroll controls scene position; it does not force snap points.
- Each scene has a poster and a normal-flow fallback for mobile/reduced motion.
- Transitions are motivated by shape, luminance, or human action—not generic crossfades.
- The strongest frame is held long enough to read before the next transition begins.
