# Netso hero craft bar

Status: reference lock and adversarial teardown complete; implementation not yet started.

## Reference lock

- **Primary direction:** The Working Roof. One continuous, directional sky-to-roof
  transformation that resolves into a stable rooftop proposition.
- **Supplied process reference:** `scroll-film-studio` v2. Preserve its one-vector
  camera rule, poster-first loading, literal frame continuity, bounded decoding, and
  mobile-as-a-different-film rule.
- **Secondary reference traits:** Supahero for immediate hero comprehension; 60fps for
  restrained motion and meaningful state changes; loadmo.re for authored mobile
  composition; Apple Environment for evidence-led narrative progression and disciplined
  media-to-copy handoffs.
- **Brand constraint:** Netso forest, warm cream, restrained gold, Archivo display,
  General Sans body, Space Mono metadata. No new visual language in this pass.

## Checkable mechanisms

1. **Proposition before spectacle**
   - At scroll position 0, the first viewport visibly contains the product category,
     target audience, Netso's role, qualification language, and one primary CTA.
   - A five-second comprehension check should identify what Netso offers and the next
     action without requiring scroll.

2. **One protagonist, one vector**
   - The roof remains the visual subject from the first sky frame through the resolved
     rooftop frame.
   - The playhead only pushes/descends toward the roof; no orbit, pullback, lateral jump,
     second film, or arbitrary scene swap is allowed.

3. **No dashboard theatre**
   - No `LIVE`, pseudo-real-time status, invented kW/PR/savings, pulsing telemetry, or
     glass value dock appears in the hero.
   - Concept media carries a same-reading-interval provenance label.

4. **Motion earns its space**
   - Desktop hero is no longer than roughly 240vh unless a measured test proves the
     extra length materially improves comprehension.
   - The first commercial qualifier appears within 1.5 viewport heights.
   - The resolved roof holds long enough to read, then hands off directly to economics.

5. **Poster is the product, motion is enhancement**
   - Poster, headline, CTA, and navigation render before optional frame decoding.
   - Failed canvas, missing frame, offline, reduced-motion, reduced-data, and Save-Data
     paths retain complete semantic copy and a working CTA in normal document flow.

6. **Mobile is authored, not cropped**
   - At 320x568, 390x667, and 390x844 there is no horizontal overflow, no important
     subject hidden by `object-fit: cover`, and no pinned 380vh desktop film.
   - Primary CTA targets are at least 44px high and the roof remains visible beside or
     above the copy.

7. **Measured lifecycle**
   - Animated frame requests are skipped in static modes and capped in desktop mode.
   - Three mount/unmount or route-cycle checks show no duplicate RAF loops,
     ScrollTriggers, pending frame pumps, or retained decoded surfaces.
   - Release evidence includes cold load, reverse scroll, fast flick, distant jump,
     resize, failed frame, blocked canvas, reduced motion, and mobile screenshots.

## Reject list

- Random full-screen preloader.
- Equal-weight hero CTAs.
- Decorative scroll cue that narrates the interaction.
- Liquid-metal or shader-dependent CTA treatment.
- Unqualified institutional claims.
- Desktop film center-cropped onto phones.
- A second cinematic controller competing with the mounted hero.

## Single biggest gap to close

The current hero has a strong visual asset but asks the visitor to wait too long for a
clear commercial proposition, then changes from a cinematic roof story into an
untrustworthy dashboard. The build must make the first state useful and the final state
quietly credible.
